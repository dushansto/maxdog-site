5. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications. They depend on notification permission, Focus, Low Power Mode, the state of your device and how iOS schedules notifications. iOS limits how many notifications an app can schedule, so MaxDog schedules reminders ahead in batches. Medication reminders are scheduled up to seven days ahead. Open MaxDog regularly, at least once a week, to keep upcoming reminders scheduled.

Widgets update on a schedule that iOS controls, so they can sometimes show older information. Open MaxDog to see the latest.

We can't guarantee that every reminder will arrive, or arrive on time. Don't rely on MaxDog alone for time-critical medication or care.
