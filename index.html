Medals

Celebrate the good days

Every task you tick off counts. Finish the day's plan and your dog earns a Good Day medal. Good days grow into weekly and monthly medals.

Open a group to see its medals.

Daily medals2 medals
  • Good DayEvery task you planned for the day, done.
  • First Good DayYour dog's very first full day.
Weekly medals3 medals
  • Steady Week5 good days in one week.
  • Strong Week6 good days in one week.
  • Perfect WeekA good day, every day of the week.
Monthly medals3 medals
  • Steady Month3 weekly medals in one month.
  • Strong Month4 weekly medals in one month.
  • Perfect Month4 weekly medals, and 3 of them are Perfect Weeks.
Monthly care medals4 medals
  • Full Bowl MonthAt least 90% of the month's meals done.
  • Happy Paws MonthAt least 90% of the month's walks done.
  • Hydration MonthAt least 90% of the month's water care done.
  • Care Keeper MonthAt least 90% of the month's care tasks done.

Each one counts when at least 20 are planned in the month.

Levels7 levels
Level 1Newcomer
Level 4Devoted Owner
Level 7Best Friend

One point for every task done, up to 12 a day. Newcomer, Companion, Reliable Owner, Devoted Owner, Guardian, Pack Leader and Best Friend.

Medals are for looking after your dog, not for hitting numbers. Rest days, changes your vet suggested and short walks on hot days all count.

Privacy

Your dog's records stay on your iPhone. No account.

There's nothing to sign up for. No MaxDog server, no cloud sync, and no ads, analytics or tracking. You decide what to share, like the health-summary PDF for your vet.

  • Kept on your deviceStored on your iPhone or iPad, not on a MaxDog server.
  • Included in your backupsCan be part of your iCloud Backup or computer backup.

Good to know

App Store prices vary by country and are shown before you subscribe. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

Still available without a subscription

Even without a subscription, you can still open:

  • Emergency information
  • Vet contacts
  • About & sources
  • Your existing medications
  • Your data: health-summary PDF and deletion

MaxDog is not a vet. It tracks, reminds and gives general guidance from published sources. It is not veterinary advice, its health content has not been individually reviewed by a veterinarian, and it doesn't calculate medication doses. In an emergency, contact your vet or an emergency clinic now.
