Privacy Policy

Your dog's data is yours.

Plain words, no surprises. Here's exactly what MaxDog collects, why, and how you stay in control.

Last updated: October 6, 2026
The short version
  • You sign in with an account, and your dog's information is stored securely in the cloud so it stays safe and in sync across your devices.
  • We use your information only to run MaxDog. We never sell it and never use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time, right in the app.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

MaxDog (“MaxDog”, “we”, “us”) is an app for dog owners, made by a small team of dog and animal lovers. If you have any questions about this policy, email us at support@maxdog.app.

2. Your account

To use MaxDog you create an account. We collect your email address and, if you choose to add it, your name. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only the details that service shares with us, such as your name and email address. Apple lets you hide your real email address if you prefer.

3. Information you add in the app

To help you care for your dog, MaxDog lets you add information such as:

  • Your dog's profile, such as name, breed, date of birth and photo
  • The care records and reminders you add

This information is linked to your account and stored on our secure cloud servers, so it stays safe and in sync across your devices. It is encrypted while it travels between your device and our servers, and access to it is restricted.

4. How we use your information

We use your information only to provide and improve MaxDog: to keep your data in sync, to send the reminders and notifications you ask for, to answer your support requests, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Service providers

We use trusted service providers to run MaxDog, such as cloud hosting and database providers that store your data on our behalf. They may only use your information to provide their service to us, under strict agreements. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own; when it is, we use the safeguards required by law, including under the GDPR.

6. Photos

If you add a photo of your dog, MaxDog uses the standard iOS photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you select. Photos are stored with your account so they appear on all your devices.

7. Notifications

MaxDog sends reminders and updates as notifications. Some are scheduled on your device, and some are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Purchases and subscriptions

MaxDog Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment, and we never receive your payment card or billing details. The app receives confirmation from Apple about whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock Premium features. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. Information Apple may share with us

If you have agreed in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as crash reports and the number of app installs. This information does not identify you, and we use it only to fix problems and improve MaxDog.

10. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and your dogs' data from our active systems within 30 days. Copies in secure backups are removed automatically on a rolling schedule.

11. Your rights and choices

You can view, edit and delete the information you've added at any time in the app, and you can delete your whole account from the app's settings. Depending on where you live, including in the EU and UK under the GDPR, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, to correct it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, email us at support@maxdog.app.

12. This website

maxdog.app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

13. Children

MaxDog is designed for dog owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will let you know in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.

15. Contact

Questions, requests or feedback? Email support@maxdog.app.
