Privacy Policy

Your dog's data is yours.

Plain words, no surprises. Here's exactly what MaxDog does, and doesn't do, with your information.

Last updated: October 5, 2026
The short version
  • Everything you enter in MaxDog is stored only on your device.
  • There's no account, and we don't run servers that receive your data.
  • We don't track you, show ads based on you, or sell or share your information.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details.

1. Who we are

MaxDog (“MaxDog”, “we”, “us”) is an app for dog owners, developed by Dushan Stojkovikj, an independent developer based in Skopje, North Macedonia. If you have any questions about this policy, email us at support@maxdog.app.

2. Information you enter in the app

To help you care for your dog, MaxDog lets you enter information such as:

  • Your dog's profile, such as name, breed, date of birth and photo
  • The care records and reminders you add

This information is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see it.

3. Information we don't collect

MaxDog does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or advertising identifier. MaxDog does not use third-party analytics or advertising tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Photos

If you choose to add a photo of your dog, MaxDog uses the standard iOS photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you select. Photos are saved on your device inside the app and are not uploaded anywhere.

5. Notifications

Any reminders you set are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No reminder content is sent to us. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

MaxDog Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment, and we never receive your payment card or billing details. The app receives confirmation from Apple about whether you have an active subscription, so it can unlock Premium features. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Information Apple may share with us

If you have agreed in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as crash reports and the number of app installs. This information does not identify you, and we use it only to fix problems and improve MaxDog.

8. Backups

Because your data lives on your device, it may be included in your regular iPhone or iPad backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer). Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

9. This website

maxdog.app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

10. Children

MaxDog is designed for dog owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Your choices and deleting your data

You are in full control of your data. You can edit or delete any record inside the app, and deleting MaxDog from your device removes all of its data from that device. Because we don't hold your data on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. But if you have any request or question about your privacy rights, including under the GDPR, just email us and we'll help.

12. Changes to this policy

MaxDog will keep growing. If we ever add a feature that needs to store information online, we will update this policy before it launches to explain clearly what changes, and the feature will be optional. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.

13. Contact

Questions, requests or feedback? Email support@maxdog.app.
