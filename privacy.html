Privacy Policy

Your dog's records stay with you.

Plain words, no surprises. This is what MaxDog 1.0 does, and doesn't do, with your information.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MaxDog is provided by:

In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the provider above. We are responsible for the personal information that reaches us, such as the support emails described in section 10.

2. The short version

  • Your dog's records are stored in the app on your device. MaxDog 1.0 has no account, no server and no cloud sync.
  • MaxDog has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We don't sell your information.
  • Apple handles payments. We never see your card details.
  • You decide what to share, for example a health-summary PDF for your vet.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to help you. We keep it for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then delete it, unless we need it for a legal claim (see section 10).

3. Your data stays on your device

MaxDog 1.0 doesn't need an account. It has no server or database run by us, no cloud sync, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It doesn't track you.

What you enter is stored in the app on your iPhone or iPad. This includes:

  • Dog profiles, such as name, birthday, breed, size and sex
  • Photos you add
  • Care records: feeding, water, walks and grooming
  • Health records: weight, allergies and conditions you note, medication, vaccinations, parasite protection and the health log
  • Reminders, expenses, medals and your settings
  • Vet and emergency contacts and a microchip number, if you type them in

MaxDog doesn't upload any of this to us. We can't see it, and we can't recover it for you, because we never hold a copy.

If you add MaxDog widgets, the app shares what they show, such as your dog's name, the next task, today's progress and water, with the widgets through storage on your device. This also stays on your device.

MaxDog doesn't read your contacts or your location. If you tap a vet's phone number or search for an emergency vet, your device opens the Phone or Maps app.

4. Photos and camera

You can add a photo of your dog in two ways:

  • From your photos. MaxDog uses the iOS photo picker, so it only receives the photos you pick. It doesn't ask for access to your whole photo library.
  • With the camera. If you allow camera access, MaxDog uses the camera only when you take a photo of your dog.

Photos are saved in the app on your device. The same applies to photos you add to the health log. You can skip photos and choose an illustration instead. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

5. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications that iOS schedules on your device. MaxDog doesn't use a push server, and no reminder content is sent to us.

Allowing notifications is optional. You can change it at any time in Settings → Notifications → MaxDog. Depending on your settings, a reminder can appear on your Lock Screen with your dog's name and, for medication reminders, the medicine name. You can choose how previews appear in Settings → Notifications. MaxDog widgets on your Home Screen or Lock Screen also show your dog's name and next task to anyone who can see your screen.

6. Purchases (Apple)

The MaxDog subscription is sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment, and we never receive your card or billing details.

On your device, MaxDog checks your purchase with Apple to unlock the app, restore purchases and show your subscription status. We don't run our own purchase server. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Apple diagnostics (opt-in)

If you choose to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple may give us crash reports and app usage statistics. Apple controls this, and you can turn it off at any time. We use this information only to fix problems and improve MaxDog.

MaxDog doesn't add its own analytics or crash-reporting tool.

8. Sharing you choose

MaxDog never sends your records anywhere on its own. Things leave the app only when you choose:

  • Health-summary PDF. You can create a PDF summary of a dog's health records and share it, for example with your vet, using the iOS share sheet. Whoever receives it gets what's in it, and their own privacy practices apply. The PDF is a summary, not a full backup of your data.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. If you ask Siri to do something in MaxDog, for example “Log water in MaxDog”, Apple handles your request under its own privacy policy, and Siri may speak or show MaxDog's answer, such as your dog's next task.
  • Links. Tapping a source, support or legal link opens the page in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

9. Backups, deleting and reinstalling

  • Backups. Your MaxDog data can be included in your iCloud Backup or in a backup on your computer, depending on your device settings. You and Apple manage these backups. MaxDog has no backup or sync service of its own.
  • Deleting. You can delete single records or a whole dog in the app. To remove everything, go to Profile → Settings → Your data → Delete all MaxDog data.
  • Deleting the app. This removes MaxDog's data from your device. It doesn't delete older device backups, and it doesn't cancel your subscription.
  • Reinstalling. Reinstalling MaxDog doesn't bring your records back. Only restoring a device backup that includes MaxDog can.

10. Support emails

If you email support@maxdog.app, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send.

  • Who handles it. support@maxdog.app is a forwarding address. Porkbun, our domain registrar, forwards your email to a Gmail inbox provided by Google, where we read and answer it. Porkbun and Google may process emails outside your country, including in the United States, under their own privacy policies.
  • Why. We use your email only to answer you and to look into the problem you report. Where data protection law asks for a legal basis, we rely on your request and on our legitimate interest in answering it. We don't use support emails for marketing, and we don't sell them.
  • How long. We keep support emails for up to 24 months after the conversation ends, and then delete them, unless we need to keep them longer to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.
  • Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your emails at any time. Just write to support@maxdog.app. See section 14 for all your rights.

Please don't send payment card details or passwords by email.

11. This website

maxdog.app is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts. When you visit, GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Children

MaxDog is made for dog owners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Future versions

MaxDog 1.0 keeps your records on your device. A future version may offer optional accounts or sync. If a future version could send your dog's records or photos off your device, we will first update this policy and explain the change in the app. Nothing will leave your device before that. If a change needs your permission or consent, we will ask first.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and similar laws in other countries, may give you rights over your personal information. These can include the right to:

  • access your information and get a copy of it
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to how it's used
  • receive it in a portable format

Your dog's records are on your device, so you control them directly: you can view, change or delete them in the app at any time. We don't hold a copy.

For information we do hold, such as support emails, write to support@maxdog.app. We'll reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the country where you live or work.
