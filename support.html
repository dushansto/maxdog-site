What is MaxDog?

+

MaxDog is a dog-care app for iPhone and iPad. It tracks feeding, water, walks, grooming, weight, medication, vaccinations, parasite protection, a health log and expenses, for up to four dogs.

It reminds you about the care you set up, gives you medals for logging care, and can create a health-summary PDF to share with your vet. Widgets and Siri let you check your dog's day and log water without opening the app. The app has four tabs: Today, Growth, Care and Profile.

How does the free trial work?

+

Eligible new subscribers can try MaxDog free for 14 days. Choose Monthly or Yearly in the app to start. You aren't charged during the trial.

If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your subscription starts and renews automatically. In the United States, it's USD 1.99 per month or USD 16.99 per year. Prices vary by country, and the App Store shows your price before you subscribe. Apple decides who is eligible for the trial.

A subscription is required after the trial. If notifications are on, MaxDog also sends a reminder before the trial ends, but please don't rely on it alone.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

+

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name (your Apple Account), then Subscriptions → MaxDog. You can also tap Manage subscription in MaxDog (Profile → Settings), or open your Apple subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Apple handles billing and refunds. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore purchases?

+

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. In MaxDog, go to Profile → Settings → Restore purchases. You'll also find Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

Restoring brings back your subscription, not your dog records. To move records, see moving to a new iPhone.

Where is my data stored?

+

On your device, inside the app. MaxDog 1.0 has no account, no server and no cloud sync, so we can't see your records and we can't restore them.

Your MaxDog data can be included in your iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer. Records don't sync between your devices, for example between your iPhone and iPad. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

I'm moving to a new iPhone. How do I keep my records?

+

Your records move with a device backup or transfer, not with your Apple Account.

  1. On your new iPhone, use Quick Start to transfer from your old iPhone, or restore from an iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer that was made after your last changes.
  2. Open MaxDog and check that your dogs are there.
  3. If the app asks you to subscribe, tap Restore purchases.

If you just download MaxDog again on a new device without a backup or transfer, it starts empty.

How do I delete my data?

+

You can delete single records or a whole dog in the app. To delete everything, go to Profile → Settings → Your data → Delete all MaxDog data. This can't be undone. If you want to keep a summary first, create a health-summary PDF on the same screen.

Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. It doesn't delete older backups, and it doesn't cancel your subscription.

My reminders aren't arriving. What can I do?

+
  • Permission. Open Settings → Notifications → MaxDog and make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  • Focus. Check that a Focus, such as Do Not Disturb or Sleep, isn't silencing MaxDog (Settings → Focus).
  • Low Power Mode. If it's on, open MaxDog now and then so it can keep your reminders up to date.
  • Open the app. iOS limits how many notifications an app can schedule, so MaxDog schedules reminders ahead in batches. Open MaxDog at least once a week to keep them scheduled.
  • Check the reminder. In MaxDog, make sure the reminder is on and set to the right time.

Reminders can't be guaranteed, so please don't rely on them alone for time-critical medication.

How do I add a MaxDog widget?

+
  1. Touch and hold your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for MaxDog and choose a size.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add MaxDog. The widget shows your dog's next task and today's progress, and you can mark a task done or log water right from it.

More than one dog? Tap your dog's name in the widget to switch, or add one widget per dog. You'll find these steps in MaxDog too: Profile → Settings → Widgets & Siri. Widgets need an active subscription.

Can I use Siri with MaxDog?

+

Yes. Try “Log water in MaxDog”, “What's next in MaxDog” or “Mark the next task done in MaxDog”. You can also name your dog, for example “What's next for Luna in MaxDog”.

On iOS 18 or later, you can add a Log water control to Control Center. Siri actions need an active subscription.

Is MaxDog a vet?

+

No. MaxDog tracks your dog's care, reminds you and gives general guidance from published sources. It is not veterinary advice, its health content has not been individually reviewed by a veterinarian, and it doesn't calculate medication doses.

If you're worried about your dog, contact your vet. In an emergency, contact your vet or an emergency clinic now.

Which devices does MaxDog work on?

+

MaxDog works on iPhone with iOS 17 or later and on iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. The Log water control in Control Center needs iOS 18 or later. MaxDog isn't available on Android yet. It's planned for later.

Which languages does MaxDog support?

+

MaxDog 1.0 is available in English.
